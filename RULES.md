# Platform Rules and Guidelines: Gyan (Indian Context)

## Introduction
Gyan is a collaborative knowledge platform dedicated to advancing homeopathy through shared clinical experiences and peer discussion among Indian homeopathic practitioners. To ensure a professional, legally compliant, and highly educational environment, we have established the following rules for Users (Registered Medical Practitioners/Students) and Administrators.

## 1. User Rules & Guidelines

### 1.1 Professional Conduct & Legal Compliance
- **Respect & Collegiality**: Treat all members with respect. Disagreements on clinical approaches, repertorization, or materia medica should be discussed constructively.
- **Evidence-Based Sharing**: When sharing clinical successes or cases, provide accurate details regarding symptoms, remedies, potencies, and follow-ups based on actual clinical experience.
- **Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954**: Users must strictly adhere to the provisions of this Act. Do not make guaranteed cure claims for diseases listed in the schedule of the Act (e.g., Cancer, Diabetes, Deafness, etc.). Posts should focus on clinical case discussions among peers, not public advertising.
- **No Spam or Self-Promotion**: Refrain from using the platform for unsolicited advertising, selling remedies, or spamming promotional content for individual clinics or pharmacies.

### 1.2 Patient Privacy & Data Protection
- **Strict Anonymity**: Never post identifiable patient information (names, contact details, recognizable photos). Always fully anonymize cases before posting to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and general medical ethics.
- **Consent**: Ensure you have the appropriate ethical consent from patients before discussing their cases, even when anonymized, in alignment with standard clinical guidelines.

### 1.3 Content Quality
- **Clear & Structured Posts**: Use the platform's provided structure for clinical posts (Symptom, Remedy, Context). Use relevant categories (e.g., Pediatrics, Dermatology, Chronic Diseases) and tags to make knowledge easily searchable.
- **Meaningful Contributions**: Whether posting a "Doubt" (seeking a second opinion for a stuck case) or answering one, aim to provide well-reasoned, helpful insights based on classical homoeopathy or established clinical experience.
- **Scientific Integrity**: Ensure discussions remain within the scope of homeopathy and medical science as recognized by the Ministry of AYUSH.

### 1.4 Account Security & Identity
- **Authentic Profiles**: Maintain an accurate professional profile. You must accurately represent your qualifications (e.g., BHMS, MD (Hom)) and your State/Central Registration Number (NCH/CCH or respective State Council).
- **Student Accounts**: Students must be actively enrolled in a recognized Homoeopathic Medical College in India.
- **Account Sharing**: Do not share your account credentials. Gyan is a trusted space for verified professionals only.

## 2. Administrator Rules & Responsibilities

### 2.1 User Verification & Onboarding
- **Vetting Process**: Admins are responsible for verifying the credentials of new users. This includes verifying BHMS/MD degrees, checking State/Central Council registration numbers, or verifying college ID cards for students.
- **Timely Approvals**: Process user verification requests promptly to ensure a smooth onboarding experience for genuine practitioners.

### 2.2 Content Moderation
- **Enforcing Guidelines**: Proactively monitor posts, comments, and doubts. Promptly remove content that violates patient privacy, makes illegal medical claims (violating the Drugs and Magic Remedies Act), or contains spam.
- **Handling Reports**: Act on community reports swiftly. If a post is flagged by users, review it objectively based on community guidelines and legal frameworks governing homoeopathy in India.
- **Neutrality**: Administer moderation without bias. Focus on the quality of discourse and adherence to rules, rather than personal clinical preferences (e.g., classical vs. clinical approaches).

### 2.3 Community Management & Support
- **Fostering Engagement**: Encourage high-quality discussions. Highlight excellent clinical cases and guide new users on how to use platform features effectively.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Mediate severe disagreements or unprofessional conduct between users fairly. Issue warnings or suspensions for repeated violations of the guidelines or the Code of Ethics of the National Commission for Homoeopathy (NCH).

### 2.4 Data Privacy & Security
- **Admin Confidentiality**: Admins must uphold the highest standards of data privacy in accordance with Indian data protection laws, ensuring user data, registration details, and platform metrics are not misused, shared, or leaked.
- **Role Management**: Ensure that platform access controls are strictly enforced, and admin privileges are only granted to trusted personnel.

---
*By adhering to these rules, Users and Admins will ensure Gyan remains a trusted, compliant, and high-quality digital hub for homeopathic excellence in India.*
